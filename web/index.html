Dijkstra Golden-Hour Referral Policy

When primary admission at NDGH fails due to capacity or specialist unavailability, the referral service evaluates all district hospitals with available matching resources and selects the destination that minimizes total ambulance transit time.

The bed and doctor at the destination hospital are atomically reserved prior to dispatch.

UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) & Healthcare Architecture
Public Health Mission
SDG 3

Good Health & Well-Being

Target 3.8: Achieve Universal Health Coverage & Emergency Care

Automated clinical triage prioritizes critical trauma cases regardless of arrival time, preventing fatal delays during the golden hour. Dijkstra shortest-path routing minimizes ambulance transit time across district hospitals.

SDG 9

Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Target 9.1: Resilient, Reliable Healthcare Digital Infrastructure

Replaces fragmented, manual paper bed registers with a high-performance system leveraging fundamental computer science data structures (PriorityQueue, HashMap, Graph, Dijkstra) running reliably without expensive proprietary software licenses.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Target 10.2 & 10.3: Equal, Bias-Free Access to Emergency Healthcare

Deterministic triage algorithms eliminate human prejudice and socio-economic favoritism from emergency bed allocation. Pooling rural and taluk hospital resources ensures equal quality of care for both urban and rural citizens.
